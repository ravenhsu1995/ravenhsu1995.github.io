More about the build +

Built with Next.js, Supabase and Vercel, using Claude Code. I’m the only developer. The repository includes 18 test files, CI and written agent guardrails requiring browser checks after interface changes.

The first check-in version was committed the night before the first session. I added cross-session profiles afterward, then the live board and question box for session two. Seven of the 32 people at the second session had attended the first.

Coaching and attendee records stay private. The product screenshots here use fictional data in an isolated environment.

May - July 2026

BeSci Wiki

An AI-queryable behavioral-science knowledge base. It reached 638 claims from 38 sources, with 70 claims checked by hand.

When an evaluation score jumped to 0.94, I checked the test set. It covered only 12 of the 79 target terms. I narrowed what the metric claimed to measure and stopped the re-extraction.

Why I stopped +

At six and a half weeks, I shut the project down. I had built infrastructure before testing whether consulting reports could be reliably mapped back to academic research. I should have started by hand-mapping three articles.

The corpus at a glance. Review states distinguish human-verified claims from drafts.

Taiwan, 2016 - 2018

Forget About Age

I co-founded an intergenerational program with a 15-person team. NSYSU’s senior university paid for a two-day camp for 21 members, designed with them through a World Café.

What the users taught us +

In the survey I ran, 19 of 20 would come again. Student helpers scored highest at 4.95 out of 5; our signature drama sessions scored lowest. Our next planned format was shorter and centered on trained student hosts.

Observe closely. Build something useful. Check what happens.
