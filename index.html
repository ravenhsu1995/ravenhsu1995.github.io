02 / The knowledge behind it

From source
to judgement.

BeSci Wiki, Second Brain and Deus Engine all serve the work of 1→0: turning research into useful human judgement. Sources stay separate from project-specific interpretations.

01 Second Brain
Keep the evidence traceable.
Immutable sources, source-linked concepts, reference checks and explicit contradictions.
02 Deus Engine
Develop an interpretation.
A workflow for reading shared knowledge, developing project-specific proposals and drafts, and reviewing them before publication.
03 1→0
Make the thinking usable.
Apply the research to problem framing, metrics and the consequences of innovation decisions.

03 / Earlier experiment · May–July 2026BeSci Wiki — archived extraction prototype+

Claims linked to sources.

An AI-queryable behavioral-science knowledge base: 638 claims from 38 sources, with 70 claims checked by hand.

Concept pages connect claims to their conditions, quotations, page numbers and source records. Review states distinguish drafts from human-verified claims.

Archived in July 2026. The evaluation covered 12 of the 79 terms it scored; extraction stopped. Corpus totals describe this earlier prototype.
