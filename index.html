Design the program. Build what it needs.

After interviewing experienced startup practitioners, I adopted OKRs for the 2026 program: individual goals, four validation workshops, and recurring consultations by field.

I initiated and independently built the dashboard. Before it, records lived in Google Forms and Excel sheets, and we matched rosters by hand. Now the team shares live progress, consultation records and next steps, with registration, follow-up and KPIs in the same system.

More about the build +

Built with Next.js, Supabase and Vercel, using Claude Code. I’m the only developer. The repository includes 18 test files and CI. Its AGENTS.md prohibits schema changes and new endpoints, and requires a browser check of every interface change.

The first check-in version was committed the night before the first session. I added cross-session profiles afterward, then the live board and question box for session two. Seven of the 32 people at the second session had attended the first.

The system tracks attendance, feedback and subsequent program-interest registrations with consent. The source repository, coaching and attendee records stay private. The product screenshots here use fictional data in an isolated environment.

May - July 2026

BeSci Wiki

Prototype closed July 2026

An AI-queryable behavioral-science knowledge base. It reached 638 claims from 38 sources, with 70 claims checked by hand.

When an evaluation score jumped from 0 to 0.94 after one fix, I checked the test set. It covered only 12 of the 79 terms it scored. I narrowed what the metric claimed to measure and stopped the re-extraction.

Why I stopped +

At six and a half weeks, I shut this extraction project down. I had built infrastructure before testing whether consulting reports could be reliably mapped back to academic research. I should have started by hand-mapping three articles. The repository remains private because it contains copyrighted research material.

The corpus at a glance. Review states distinguish human-verified claims from drafts.

Separate the source
from the interpretation.

BeSci's retrospective became a starting point for Deus Engine. I later separated the shared knowledge from each project's interpretation and outputs.

Second Brain
A separate knowledge repository: immutable sources, source-linked concept pages and a reference checker. Project-specific interpretations stay outside it.
Deus Engine
A research-to-content workflow that reads Second Brain, develops project-specific interpretations and brings proposals and drafts to me for review.
1→0
A separate platform applying humanities and social science to innovation. It draws on Second Brain and references Deus's workflow, while keeping its data and runtime independent.

Taiwan, 2016 - 2018

Forget About Age

I co-founded an intergenerational program with a 15-person team. NSYSU’s senior university paid for a two-day camp for 21 members, designed with them through a World Café.

What the users taught us +

In the survey I ran, 19 of 20 would come again. Student helpers scored highest at 4.95 out of 5; our signature drama sessions scored lowest. Our next planned format was shorter and centered on trained student hosts.

Observe closely. Build something useful. Check what happens.
