How I work

Open the door first

Merton's Matthew effect says advantage follows advantage. The founders who need help most often haven't applied yet, so our open office hours let anyone in and meet them early.

Measure what founders actually do

In my "Garage 2.0" proposal I argued that we were judging 0→1 teams by accelerator metrics, and that our in-or-out gate meant students who weren't ready simply left.

Ask the people it's for

In 2016 I co-founded Forget About Age, an intergenerational program. Before our two-day camp in June 2017, we ran a World Café with the senior-university members it was for. Afterward, their survey rated our student helpers 4.95/5, while the drama sessions we had pitched as our signature came last.

Build it with agents, and kill it if it's wrong

I build with Claude Code. 5 of my 7 main repos have an AGENTS.md or CLAUDE.md with rules for the agents. The dashboard's AGENTS.md fences them off (no schema changes, no new endpoints, no new UI libraries) and requires a browser check of every UI change: screenshots in light and dark mode, KPI text read back, console errors scanned. The dashboard and BeSci both have CI. When I realized BeSci's mistake was an untested premise, I shut it down and wrote up why. On the next project I set the bar before running anything.
