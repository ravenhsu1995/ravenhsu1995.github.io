01 / NTU Garage

NTU Garage.
Founder Pipeline.

The software our staff use to run NTU Garage, our early-stage founder program, and NTUTEC Innovation Support (創創相談室), the open monthly meetups that feed founders into Garage. I built it alone, without being asked, so our staff no longer match names by hand across Google Forms and spreadsheets.

Behind the program

Running NTU Garage.

Our staff see each Garage team’s live progress, consultation records and next steps in one place. A rules engine manages every course and consultation, closes tasks automatically and flags teams that miss three sessions in a row.

02 / Travel Fox · Live product

Travel Fox.
The plan is the budget.

A trip-planning web app I built for my own Kansai trip and opened to anyone who signs up. Costs entered while planning roll straight into the trip budget alongside expenses logged during the trip, converted at daily exchange rates, so the plan and the spending never drift apart.

17 days

The knowledge behind it

From source
to judgement.

I built 1→0 and, with AI agents, the research knowledge base its writing draws on. The knowledge base began as BeSci Wiki; the current version is Second Brain.

01 BeSci Wiki
Extract claims from research.
Claims extracted from papers and reports, each linked to its source.
02 Second Brain
Keep the evidence traceable.
Every claim must cite a registered source, and a model from a different AI company cross-checks full-text readings against the originals, catching misattributed claims.
03 1→0
Make the thinking usable.
Its writing draws on the knowledge base for problem framing, metrics and the consequences of innovation decisions.

First version · May–July 2026BeSci Wiki — where the knowledge base began+

Claims linked to sources.

A behavioral-science knowledge base: 638 claims from 38 sources, each traceable to its source.

I paired the extracting model with a second model as reviewer and measured the extraction against a 25-document gold set I checked by hand. Concept pages connect claims to their conditions, quotations, page numbers and source records.

When the extraction’s term-tagging score jumped from 0 to 0.94, I found the gold set covered only 12 of the 79 terms, so I stopped treating it as a quality score and halted a full re-extraction that relied on it. Its sources and lessons carried into Second Brain.

The corpus at a glance. Review states distinguish human-verified claims from drafts.

Taiwan, 2016–2018

Forget About Age

As CEO, I led a 6-person core team building an intergenerational camp service that pairs college students with older adults. We sold a two-day camp to NSYSU’s senior university and designed it with participants through a World Café.

In the post-camp survey, 95% would return and participants rated the camp 4.55/5 overall. They rated the student hosts 4.95/5, the highest score they gave.
